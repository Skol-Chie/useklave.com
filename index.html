FAQ

Before you start

Can I create both free and paid groups?

Yes. Most creators use a free group to build trust, then move serious students into paid cohorts or premium coaching rooms.

Do students need to install an app?

No. Klave works in the browser and can be saved to a phone home screen for a native-feeling experience.

When do creators get paid?

Your wallet updates as students join paid groups. From there, you can withdraw using the payout options available in your account.

What makes Klave different from a normal chat app?

Klave combines group chat, course discovery, payments, wallets, profiles, and creator growth tools in one focused workspace.
